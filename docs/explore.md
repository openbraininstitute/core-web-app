# Explore

The Explore section is your gateway to discovering experimental data, computational models, simulations, and scientific literature. It provides powerful tools to navigate through brain regions and find relevant resources for your research.

## Overview

The Explore section offers two main modes of discovery:

1. **Interactive Exploration**: Navigate brain regions and discover associated data, models, and literature
2. **Knowledge Discovery**: Explore scientific publications and query literature using AI-powered tools

## Interactive Exploration

Interactive exploration allows you to browse data organized by brain regions using an interactive 3D brain atlas.

### Brain Region Navigation

The platform features an interactive 3D brain atlas that lets you:

- **Select Brain Regions**: Click on regions in the 3D viewer or use the hierarchical selector to choose specific brain regions
- **Navigate the Hierarchy**: Browse through the brain region hierarchy from high-level structures down to specific regions
- **Filter by Region**: All data, models, and simulations are automatically filtered based on your selected brain region

### Data Types

The exploration interface organizes content into three main categories:

#### Experimental Data

Browse experimental measurements and observations:

- **Neuron Density**: Experimental measurements of neuron density in different brain regions
- **Bouton Density**: Synaptic bouton density measurements
- **Synapses Per Connection**: Experimental data on synaptic connectivity
- **Cell Morphology**: Reconstructed neuron morphologies from experimental data
- **Electrical Cell Recordings**: Electrophysiological recordings from neurons
- **Ion Channel Recordings**: Experimental ion channel data

#### Models

Explore computational models at different scales:

- **Circuits**: Multi-neuron circuit models
- **E-models**: Single neuron electrical models
- **ME-models**: Multi-compartment neuron models
- **ME-models with Synapses**: Circuit models incorporating synaptic mechanisms
- **Single Neuron Synaptomes**: Models of individual neurons with detailed synaptic information
- **Ion Channel Models**: Models of ion channel dynamics

#### Simulations

Browse simulation results:

- **Single Neuron Simulations**: Simulations of individual neurons
- **Circuit Simulations**: Simulations of neural circuits
- **Microcircuit Simulations**: Simulations of small neural networks
- **Synaptome Simulations**: Simulations incorporating detailed synaptic information

### Data Scoping

You can explore data in two scopes:

- **Public**: Browse publicly available data, models, and simulations shared by the research community
- **Project**: Access data, models, and simulations within your project workspace

Use the scope selector at the top of the data view to switch between public and project data.

### Browsing Data

Once you've selected a brain region and data type:

1. **View Data Cards**: Each data type is displayed as a card showing the number of available items
2. **Browse by Type**: Click on a data type card to see all available items of that type
3. **Filter and Search**: Use filters and search tools to find specific items
4. **View Details**: Click on any item to see detailed information, metadata, and associated resources

### Data Details

When viewing a specific data item, you can:

- **View Metadata**: See comprehensive information about the data, including experimental conditions, brain regions, and associated publications
- **Explore Related Data**: Discover related experimental data, models, and simulations
- **Access Publications**: View scientific publications associated with the data
- **Download Data**: Download data files for offline analysis
- **View Visualizations**: Explore interactive visualizations of the data

## Knowledge Discovery

The Knowledge Discovery feature helps you explore scientific literature and publications related to brain regions and experimental data.

### Literature Exploration

- **Browse Publications**: Explore scientific papers associated with brain regions and experimental data
- **AI-Powered Queries**: Use the chatbot interface to ask questions about publications and get summaries
- **Contextual Information**: Discover literature relevant to specific brain regions or data types

### Using the AI Assistant

The platform includes an AI assistant that can:

- Answer questions about available data
- Summarize scientific publications
- Help you understand relationships between different data types
- Provide insights about brain regions and their associated research

Access the AI assistant from the workspace interface to start exploring literature and getting answers to your research questions.

## Tips for Effective Exploration

1. **Start Broad**: Begin by selecting a high-level brain region to see all available data
2. **Narrow Down**: Use the brain region hierarchy to focus on specific areas of interest
3. **Switch Data Types**: Explore different tabs (Experimental, Models, Simulations) to get a comprehensive view
4. **Use Filters**: Apply filters to find specific types of data or models
5. **Check Related Items**: When viewing an item, explore related data and publications for additional context
6. **Save Bookmarks**: Bookmark interesting items for quick access later

## Integration with Other Features

The Explore section integrates seamlessly with other platform features:

- **Workflows**: Use discovered models and data as starting points for building your own models
- **Simulations**: Reference experimental data when configuring simulations
- **Notebooks**: Access data directly from notebooks for custom analyses
- **Reports**: Include explored data and models in your project reports

The Explore section is designed to be your starting point for discovering resources and building your research workflows on the platform.
